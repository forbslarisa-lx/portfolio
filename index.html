About Me

I specialize in translating complex knowledge into clear, usable learning. My work combines instructional design, pedagogy, and knowledge translation. Across projects, I focus on transforming complex knowledge - academic, technical, and sensitive, into structured content - accurate and accessible, for effective learning experiences.

Across such different contexts, the core challenge is the same: preserving the integrity of complex knowledge while making it understandable, structured, and applicable in real-world situations.
